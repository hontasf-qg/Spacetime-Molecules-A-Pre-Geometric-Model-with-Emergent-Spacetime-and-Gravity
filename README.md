## Abstract. ## 
We construct a background-free model in which spacetime is built from two species of fermionic spinor "atoms": left-handed atoms, written ${/}$, and their right-handed mirror partners, written ${\backslash}$. There is no ambient space; adjacency and geometry are properties of the atoms' states. A pair of atoms forms a bosonic *molecule* ${X}={/}{\backslash}$, and an elementary molecule is a null vector. Two flavors of atoms reconstruct a null tetrad and the Minkowski metric, so the atoms fix the local light cone and leave only a conformal scale free. The dynamics is a Wheeler--DeWitt constraint, written as a balance ${\hat{T}}|\Psi\rangle =-{\hat{V}}|\Psi\rangle$ between the binding ${\hat{V}}$ of neighboring atoms and the transfer ${\hat{T}}$ of excitations between neighboring patches. For fixed $SU(2)$ links we solve the constraint exactly. Its solutions are the molecular states, the zero-point energies of the two species cancel, the molecules are hard-core bosons that obey the oscillator algebra when dilute, and phase-coherent molecular condensates, including a thermofield double, are physical states. In a curved region the condensate's order parameter is the timelike normal of the spatial slice on each patch, while light rays are carried by the bonds. The holonomies of ${\hat{T}}$ reproduce the Cartan structure equations, and the curvature-squared functionals built from them include Starobinsky and MacDowell--Mansouri gravity. If one of these governs the continuum, the model implies two tensor gravitational-wave polarizations and a scalaron range below $10^{-4}$ m, and an $R^2$ inflaton gives $n_s\simeq0.96$--$0.97$ and $r\simeq0.003$--$0.005$. Which action the dynamics selects is open. The algebraic results are machine-checked in Lean 4 and Rocq, and every result is reproduced in a companion notebook.

## The physical picture. ## 
Taken together, these results suggest a picture of spacetime as a molecular superfluid. Fermionic chiral spinor atoms ${/}$ and ${\backslash}$, with no space around them, are paired by the constraint into bosonic molecules ${X}={/}{\backslash}$. The molecules obey the oscillator algebra and condense, and the condensate's order parameter carries the local light cone. The atoms' static vacuum energies cancel, and the information flow between patches carries the curvature. If the observed de Sitter asymptotics are imposed, this superfluid fits a dS/CFT framework in which gravity and the Standard-Model fields appear as boundary operators. Its superfluid dynamics (phase stiffness, collective modes) and the selection of a gravitational action remain to be derived. The picture is nonetheless falsifiable. Each of the following would rule out a specific part of it:

-   a third gravitational-wave polarization at detector frequencies;

-   a scalaron range above $0.1$ mm;

-   a tensor-to-scalar ratio outside $r\simeq0.003$--$0.005$ (if the $R^2$ channel is the inflaton);

-   a confirmed departure from $w=-1$.
